# Frontier AI Engineering Academy

A public, hands-on learning platform for building the skills and portfolio needed to pursue engineering and research-oriented roles at **Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and OpenAI**.

**Live site:** https://dipayandey1244.github.io/ai-engineering-academy/

## What this is

This is not a generic AI overview. The academy is designed as a progressive sequence: understand a concept, connect it to public research or engineering practice, implement it, test it, explain trade-offs, and produce portfolio evidence.

Company-specific material is based on public papers, official engineering posts, documentation, job descriptions, and reproducible experiments. We do not claim access to confidential internal systems or hiring criteria.

## Learning loop

1. **Learn:** intuitive explanations, math, diagrams, and worked examples.
2. **Connect:** read relevant papers and official technical posts.
3. **Implement:** build working components in Python and PyTorch.
4. **Evaluate:** test correctness, quality, reliability, latency, and cost.
5. **Explain:** answer conceptual, debugging, and research questions.
6. **Publish:** maintain a reproducible repository and a clear technical report.

## Curriculum roadmap

1. AI engineering orientation and frontier-lab landscape
2. Python, NumPy, tensors, autograd, optimizers, and training loops
3. Deep learning fundamentals and debugging
4. Transformers, tokenization, attention, and a tiny language model
5. Training systems, profiling, and efficiency
6. Post-training, reinforcement learning, and reasoning
7. Agents, tool use, orchestration, and workflow design
8. Evaluation science, reliability, and safety
9. Interpretability and model behavior
10. Research reproduction, ablations, and a flagship project
11. Coding, ML, systems, and research interviews
12. Role-specific applications and outreach

Modules are marked available only when their lessons and exercises have been built. A roadmap entry is not a claim that the material is already complete.

## Target role families

- **Google DeepMind:** research engineering, deep learning, reinforcement learning, world models, multimodal systems, scientific experimentation.
- **Anthropic:** model behavior, interpretability, evaluations, agents, reliability, and safety-oriented engineering.
- **OpenAI:** foundation-model engineering, post-training, reasoning, inference, evaluations, and scalable AI systems.

These are broad public-facing research and engineering themes, not a claim that every team or role works on every topic. Target role requirements vary; always compare the curriculum against current job descriptions.

## Site features

- Responsive lesson navigation and research companion
- Exercises with hints and revealable reference answers
- Diagnostic assessment and scored feedback
- Research bibliography with reading filters
- Local progress, bookmarks, and reading state stored in your browser

Progress is stored locally in your browser and is not synchronized across devices.

## Run locally

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site. Open `index.html` in a browser or serve the repository root with any static web server. No build step or API key is required.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages deploys the repository root through the workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml`.

## Guiding principle

The objective is to build demonstrable competence—not to promise a job. Every substantial module should culminate in code, tests, measured results, a technical explanation, and a clear statement of limitations.
